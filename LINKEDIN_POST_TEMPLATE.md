## 📱 Suggested LinkedIn Post Copy

**Excited to share my latest project: Gather & Co. — An AI-Powered Celebration & Event Planning Studio 🥂✨**

Built for **PromptWars Challenge 05**, Gather & Co. is designed to turn celebration ideas into organized, budget-aware, producer-grade event plans in seconds.

Whether planning a milestone 21st birthday, a private dinner, a wedding reception, or a corporate gathering, the hardest part is often balancing budgets, timelines, catering, and decorations without getting overwhelmed.

Here is how Gather & Co. solves this:

1️⃣ **Guided Event Studio:** Rather than filling out generic inputs, hosts specify occasion details, target guests, and financial caps with instant per-guest budget intelligence.
2️⃣ **Producer Workspace & Visual Budget Strip:** Real-time proportional visualizer tracking catering, venue, decor, sound, and contingency reserves.
3️⃣ **Comprehensive Event Dossiers:**
   • Chronological run-of-show schedule with milestone timestamps
   • Budget breakdown tables itemized in Indian Rupees (₹)
   • Multi-course culinary recommendations tailored to dietary preferences
   • Theme & visual atmosphere styling
   • Step-by-step preparation checklists
   • Letterpress-styled WhatsApp/digital invitation preview
   • Contingency & risk mitigation protocols
4️⃣ **Zero-Dependency Local Persistence:** Full in-browser plan management with instant save, view, export, and non-blocking deletion.

**Tech Stack:**
• Frontend: React 19, TypeScript, Vite, Vanilla CSS with custom editorial design system (warm ivory, deep forest green, and terracotta)
• Backend: Express.js, Node.js
• Intelligence: Google Gemini API integration with smart localized plan synthesis
